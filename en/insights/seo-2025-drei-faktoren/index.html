SEO 2025: these 3 factors matter more than keywords

SEO in 2025 is no longer just about keywords. The three factors that now decide whether you stay visible on Google – and how to use them for your business.

  • SEO & GEO

The days when a few cleverly placed keywords got you onto page one of Google are over. In 2025, one thing counts above all: relevance for real people. In this article I’ll show you three decisive factors that matter more today than classic keyword optimisation – and how you can use them for your business.

1. Understand search intent – and meet it

Google is better than ever at recognising the intent behind a search. Content that doesn’t match the search intent gets penalised – no matter how often the keyword appears.

Example:
Someone searching for “best project management tools” expects a comparison or a list – not a sales page for a single tool.

Tip:

Analyse the top results for your target keywords. Which formats dominate? What does the user expect? Create content that meets exactly those expectations.

2. E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authority, trust

Google increasingly rates content by the E-E-A-T principle:

  • Experience: Do you have hands-on experience with the topic?
  • Expertise: Are you a recognised expert?
  • Authoritativeness: Do others cite your site as a source?
  • Trustworthiness: Is your site secure and transparent?

Tip:

Show real experience, cite expert sources, present customer testimonials and make sure your website is secure and transparent.

3. User experience (UX) and user signals

Google measures how users interact with your site:

  • Time on page: How long do visitors stay?
  • Bounce rate: Do they leave again quickly?
  • Interactions: Do they click links, share content or fill in forms?

Tip:

Optimise your site for an excellent user experience:

  • Fast loading times
  • Clear navigation
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • High-quality, relevant content

Conclusion

In 2025, SEO is no longer just about keywords but about real value for the user. If you understand search intent, build trust and offer an excellent user experience, you’ll be rewarded – with better rankings and more visibility.
