## Situation

Volvo wanted US customers to configure and order cars online – at a time when most manufacturers had just put a brochure on the web. Plus a link to Volvo Finance for financing right inside the order process.

## Solution

A new platform with a store, 3D renderings of every model for configuration, dealer search and a financing flow. Aligned with the agency’s creative directors and with Volvo/Ford.

## Execution

At Euro RSCG MVBMS in New York I was senior technical director, responsible for the technical build and the entire production, including the 3D renderings. In parallel, the relaunch of the New Balance website: e-commerce, multilingual, for several sales regions – $2.5 million.

## Result

Both platforms went live. What I took away: big budgets don’t solve problems you haven’t described properly first. That’s as true in 2026 as it was in 2000 – except that today a team of ten with AI does what took 120 of us back then.
