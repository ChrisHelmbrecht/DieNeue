## Situation

A German school abroad competes for two groups who both come from far away: families moving to Moscow looking for a school for their children, and teachers from Germany going abroad for a few years. Both decide before they ever see the building – based on what they find online.

## Solution

Show, don’t describe. A series of films about everyday school life: primary school, lessons, teacher portraits, celebrations, the schoolyard. Plus Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn with regular glimpses, and a website that gets parents and applicants quickly to what they need.

## Execution

Concept, shoot, edit and running the channels over several years, with a small team on site. The films are still on YouTube today – a few of them are in the slider on the home page.

## Result

An online presence that shows the school as it is, regularly named at parents’ evenings and in job interviews as the reason people got in touch.
