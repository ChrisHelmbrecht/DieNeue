## Situation

Estonian investors had taken over a five-star hotel in the old town of Lviv. Good house, interchangeable presence: the branding said nothing about the place, nothing about the house, nothing about why you should stay here and not next door.

## Solution

An identity that comes from the city – Lviv, Austro-Hungarian history, coffee-house tradition – and runs through everything: logo, stationery, brochures and menus, website, the things in the room.

## Execution

Led by my agency Kollektiv in Moscow, together with a creative director from Landor for brand strategy and design. Plus a hotel film that now runs in the slider on the home page.

## Result

The Leopolis still uses the identity today, more than a decade later. Branding that lasts is the best argument for branding.
