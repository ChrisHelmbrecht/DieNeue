## Situation

VGAN makes dairy-free chocolate that most people can’t tell from the original – 44% cocoa, oat milk, organic. Where it sits on US shelves, it outsells its category. The problem is the shelf itself: “plant-based” on the front of the pack tells the shopper, before they’ve tasted it, that it tastes worse.

Plus the usual start-up conditions: small team in Norway, big market in the US, a budget that doesn’t stretch to classic advertising.

## Solution

Move to the premium shelf. New name, new identity, the chocolate stays as it is. US launch in 2027, taking existing customers and retailers along.

My part as head of marketing: positioning and brand architecture, website and content, social media, launch preparation – and the question of how to do that with a budget no agency would touch.

## Execution

AI here isn’t a nice-to-have, it’s the reason the team can be this small: market research, competitor analysis, copy variants in several languages, image drafts, first packaging ideas – all faster and cheaper than before. What has to be real stays real: photography, tastings, conversations with retailers.

In autumn 2026 we set the case at BI Norwegian Business School in Oslo: how do you take a brand to the US under a new name? After a week of research the winning team arrived at almost exactly our plan. Reassuring – and a few ideas we’re adopting. [More in the article.](/en/insights/bi-case-competition)

## Result

The launch is in preparation; the name will be announced in 2027. Numbers follow once the chocolate sits on the new shelf.
