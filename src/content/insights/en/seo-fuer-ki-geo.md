The way people search for information online is changing fast. Artificial intelligence (AI) is revolutionising search: instead of classic search results, AI-powered platforms like **ChatGPT**, **Perplexity**, **Microsoft Copilot** and **Google Gemini** give direct answers to users' questions. For content creators and SEO experts this means: new strategies are needed to stay visible in this new search landscape.

## What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is a new approach in SEO that aims to shape content so that generative AI systems recognise it and include it in their answers. Unlike traditional SEO, which focuses on keyword rankings and backlinks, GEO concentrates on optimising content for AI-driven queries.

GEO takes the specific requirements of AI systems into account and aims to shape content so that these systems prefer to select and present it. This includes factors such as how content is structured, the use of clear and precise language, and the integration of trustworthy sources.

## How do AI search engines work?

## ChatGPT

OpenAI's ChatGPT uses large language models to generate answers based on training data and current information from the web. It prefers content that:

- radiates **authority and trustworthiness**
- is **up to date**
- is presented **clearly and in a structured way**

## Perplexity

Perplexity combines traditional search engine technology with AI to deliver precise answers. The platform puts particular emphasis on:

- **relevance** of the content
- **quality** of the information
- **trustworthy sources**

## Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft Copilot integrates AI into the Office suite and Bing search to offer users context-based support. For SEO, this means shaping content so that it is understandable and helpful in different contexts.

## Google Gemini

Google Gemini is Google's answer to AI-powered search. It combines traditional Google search with AI-generated summaries. Important factors for visibility are:

- **E-E-A-T** (experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness)
- **structured data**
- **user intent**

## Strategies to optimise your content for AI search engines

1. **Increase the authority of your content**
   - Publish well-founded articles with clear sources.
   - Use structured data (Schema.org) to make the context of your content clear.
2. **Focus on user intent**
   - Understand which questions your audience has, and answer them precisely.
   - Avoid keyword stuffing; use natural language instead.
3. **Keep your content up to date**
   - Update your content regularly to keep it relevant.
   - Take current trends and developments in your industry into account.
4. **Optimise your website's technical performance**
   - Fast loading times and mobile optimisation are crucial.
   - Avoid excessive scripts and large image files that can hurt performance.
5. **Use structured data**
   - Implement structured data to make it easier for AI systems to understand your content.
   - This can increase the chance of being included in AI-generated answers.

## Conclusion

The integration of AI into search engines is changing the rules of SEO. To stay visible in this new landscape, it is crucial to optimise content not only for traditional search engines but also for AI systems. By focusing on authority, relevance, freshness and technical excellence, you can make sure your content is found and used in the era of AI search.
